Lens Photo Shot List

Recyclopedia · photo scanner benchmark

Forty photos, one object each

The photo scanner is built and switched off until we know how often it is right on real phone photos. This is the shot list: what to photograph, how, and what to avoid, with an openly licensed photo of each as an example. Your photos should look less tidy than the examples: a real counter, a real garage, whatever light the room has.

0 of 40 taken

Before you start

  1. Turn off location in photos. iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → Never. The folder must not carry your home address.
  2. Save as JPEG. iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.
  3. One object, filling about two thirds of the frame. Normal 1× camera, no zoom, no portrait mode, no filters.
  4. Whatever light the room has. No flash.
  5. Nothing personal in frame. No faces, no addresses on labels, no lit screens.
  6. Name each file as shown on its card (e.g. 01-aluminum-can.jpg). The name is the answer the scanner has to find, so a card and its file must match.
  7. Drop them in Google Drive: RandDRecyclopedia/lens-fixtures/. They never go into the public repository.

Metal 4 shots

0101-aluminum-can.jpg

An empty drink can, standing up.

How
Stand it on the kitchen counter. Shoot from the side at can height so the shape and the ring pull both show.
Avoid
Crushing it first. A crushed can is a different photo.
0202-steel-tin-can.jpg

An empty food can (soup, beans, tomatoes), label on or off.

How
Rinse it, stand it up, shoot from slightly above so the rim shows. Holding it in your hand is fine.
Avoid
A lid still hanging off by a sharp edge. Press it in first.
0303-aerosol-can-empty.jpg

An empty spray can (air freshener, cooking spray, deodorant).

How
Upright, cap on, on a counter or shelf. Fill the frame top to bottom.
Avoid
Pressing the nozzle to check it is empty while you shoot.
0404-scrap-metal.jpg

A piece of scrap metal: an old pipe, bracket, hinge, a pan without its handle.

How
Lay it on the garage floor or a workbench. One piece, or a few of the same kind.
Avoid
A mixed pile of metal and other junk.

Plastic 5 shots

0505-plastic-bottle-pet.jpg

A clear plastic water or soda bottle (#1 PET), cap on.

How
Stand it up against a plain wall. Clear plastic hides against busy backgrounds.
Avoid
A window behind it: the bottle disappears into the light.
0606-plastic-jug-hdpe.jpg

A milk or juice jug (#2 HDPE), rinsed.

How
Side view with the handle visible. The kitchen counter is fine.
Avoid
Showing only the bottom with the recycling number.
0707-plastic-bag-film.jpg

A plastic shopping bag.

How
Lay it flat on a dark table so its edges show, or hold it up by the handles.
Avoid
A bag stuffed inside another bag.
0808-styrofoam-foam-ps.jpg

A foam cup, tray or packing block (#6 polystyrene).

How
On a dark surface: white foam vanishes on a white counter.
Avoid
Crumbs of foam everywhere. One piece, whole or broken.
0909-yogurt-container-pp.jpg

A yogurt cup or tub (#5 PP).

How
Empty and rinsed, lid on or beside it. Shoot at a slight angle so the depth shows.
Avoid
Shooting it on a store shelf among many others (the example does; yours should not).

Paper 4 shots

1010-cardboard-clean-dry.jpg

A clean, dry cardboard box.

How
A shipping box on the floor or held in one hand. Tape on is fine.
Avoid
Your name and address on the shipping label. Peel it off or turn the box around.
1111-newspaper.jpg

A newspaper, or a small stack of them.

How
Folded, on a table. A stack is fine if it is only newspaper.
Avoid
Mixing in magazines or mail.
1212-pizza-box-clean-top-half.jpg

A pizza box, the clean top half if you have one.

How
Closed, from above, on the counter or floor.
Avoid
An open box full of leftovers (that is organics, not this shot).
1313-paper-coffee-cup.jpg

A disposable paper coffee cup, lid on.

How
Stand it on a table and shoot from the side at cup height.
Avoid
A reusable mug: it is a different item.

Glass 3 shots

1414-glass-bottle-clear.jpg

A clear glass bottle.

How
Upright against a plain wall, like the PET bottle. A label is fine.
Avoid
A bottle with a drink still in it.
1515-glass-food-jar.jpg

A glass food jar (jam, pickles, pasta sauce), lid on.

How
On the counter, at a slight angle so the lid and the glass both show.
Avoid
A plastic jar that looks like glass. Tap it if unsure.
1616-ceramics-pottery.jpg

A ceramic mug or plate.

How
One mug on a table, handle to the side.
Avoid
A glass mug: ceramics are the point of this shot.

Electronics 4 shots

1717-smartphone.jpg

A smartphone, screen off.

How
Lay it on a plain table, screen up, at a slight angle.
Avoid
A lit screen showing messages, photos or notifications.
1818-laptop-notebook-computer.jpg

A laptop, lid open.

How
On a desk, shot from the front and a little above.
Avoid
Anything personal on the screen. Close apps or turn it off.
1919-keyboard-mouse.jpg

A computer keyboard, a mouse, or both.

How
From above on the desk, the whole keyboard in frame.
Avoid
Cutting off one end of the keyboard.
2020-charging-cables-cords.jpg

A charging cable, coiled.

How
Coil it loosely on a table so both connectors show.
Avoid
A tangle of several cables (a bundle is a different shot).

Batteries 4 shots

2121-alkaline-battery-aa-aaa-9v.jpg

AA or AAA alkaline batteries (a few).

How
Two to four batteries on a table, labels up. The example shows rechargeable AAs: same framing; the printed label is what tells them apart.
Avoid
Batteries still inside a remote or toy.
2222-button-coin-cell.jpg

A coin cell (CR2032 or similar).

How
Get close: it is small. Tap the screen to focus and keep it in the middle of the frame.
Avoid
Shooting from far away, where it becomes a dot.
2323-car-battery-lead-acid.jpg

A car battery.

How
In the garage, from the front and a little above so the terminals show.
Avoid
Touching both terminals, or moving the battery only to take a photo.
2424-power-tool-battery.jpg

A power-tool battery pack (drill, trimmer).

How
Off the tool, on a workbench, label side up.
Avoid
Leaving it on the tool: then it is a drill.

Hazardous 4 shots

2525-paint-latex-water-based.jpg

A can of paint.

How
Lid on, on the garage floor, from above at an angle so the lid and label both show.
Avoid
Opening a can just for the photo.
2626-category-hazardous.jpg

A household cleaner in its bottle (spray, bleach, drain cleaner).

How
Upright on the counter or under the sink, label facing the camera.
Avoid
Opening or mixing cleaners. Never pour one into another.
2727-fluorescent-bulb-cfl.jpg

A compact fluorescent bulb (the curly or folded-tube kind).

How
Switched off, on a table, lying down or standing on its base.
Avoid
A broken bulb. If one breaks, air out the room and skip this shot.
2828-motor-oil.jpg

A motor oil bottle.

How
Cap on, upright on the garage floor or a shelf.
Avoid
Oil dripping down the outside. Wipe the bottle first.

Textiles 3 shots

2929-clothing-wearable.jpg

A piece of clothing someone could still wear (T-shirt, sweater).

How
Folded, or laid flat on a bed or table, filling the frame.
Avoid
Your feet or legs in the shot (the example has them; yours should not).
3030-shoes.jpg

A pair of shoes.

How
Side by side on the floor, from the side and a little above.
Avoid
One shoe on a foot.
3131-textiles-fabric-scraps.jpg

Fabric scraps or rags.

How
A small pile on a table.
Avoid
Clothes that still work: those are shot 29.

Organics 2 shots

3232-food-scraps-organics.jpg

Food scraps: peels, cores, coffee grounds.

How
In a bowl or compost bin, from above.
Avoid
Packaging mixed into the scraps.
3333-category-organics.jpg

A piece of plain wood: an offcut, a plank, a pallet slat.

How
On the floor, the whole piece in frame.
Avoid
Painted or treated wood (that has a different answer).

Rubber 2 shots

3434-tires.jpg

A car tire.

How
Lying flat or leaning against a wall, the whole tire in frame.
Avoid
A tire still on the car.
3535-category-rubber.jpg

A garden hose.

How
Loosely coiled on the ground.
Avoid
A hose on its reel: the reel becomes the subject.

Bulky 2 shots

3636-mattress.jpg

A mattress.

How
Leaning against a wall, the whole mattress in frame. Step back.
Avoid
Shooting only a corner.
3737-category-bulky-goods.jpg

A big piece of furniture: an armchair, sofa or desk chair.

How
The whole piece, from a few steps back.
Avoid
People sitting on it.

Deliberately bad 3 shots

3838-aluminum-can.jpg

BLURRY: the drink can from shot 01.

How
Move the phone as you press the shutter. The can is still the only thing in the frame, just smeared.
Avoid
A different object. The label stays aluminum-can, so it has to be a can.
3939-alkaline-battery-aa-aaa-9v.jpg

DARK: the AA batteries from shot 21.

How
Same framing as shot 21, in a dim room, flash off.
Avoid
Pure black. Dim, not dark.
4040-smartphone.jpg

CLUTTERED: your phone in a messy drawer or on a messy desk.

How
Put the phone somewhere busy (junk drawer, crowded desk) and shoot it where it lies, roughly in the middle of the frame.
Avoid
Letters, papers or screens that can be read.
